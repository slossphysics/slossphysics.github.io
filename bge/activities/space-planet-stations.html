Planet stations

Nine station cards, one for each planet and one for the dwarf planets. Pupils go round the stations and fill in a table: the distance from the Sun, whether the planet is rocky or a gas giant, and one interesting fact.

  1. Print the station cards in colour. Each card fills one A4 page (landscape).
  2. Print one pupil table for each pupil.
  3. Put the cards round the room, numbered 1 to 9 in order from the Sun.
  4. The cards don't say rocky or gas giant: pupils decide from the Surface line.
