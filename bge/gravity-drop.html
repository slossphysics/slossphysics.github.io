BGE · Third level · Forces, electricity and waves · SCN 3-08a

Gravity drop

LI: I am learning how gravity makes things fall on different planets.

SC: I can predict which object lands first · I can explain why objects in the same gravity land together · I can explain why things fall faster where gravity is stronger.

Quick set-ups:

Things to try

  1. Predict first: on the Moon, which lands first, a feather or an elephant? Then drop them.
  2. Drop the same object on Earth, Mars and Jupiter. Put the places in order, fastest first. Why is it that order?
  3. Find two different planets where things land at exactly the same time. What do they have in common?
  4. Turn the air on. What happens to the feather on Earth? What about on the Moon? Why?
  5. Find the place where things land fastest, and a place where they never land at all.
  6. Look at the mass and the weight under each lane. Which one changes when you change the place?

Tap a lane to drop just that one. The planets' gravity is rounded to whole numbers, the same as in class.
