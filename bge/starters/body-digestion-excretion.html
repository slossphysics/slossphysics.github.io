Digestion and excretion

LI: I am learning how the body breaks down food and gets rid of waste.

SC: I can put the parts of the digestive system in order · I can say where food and water are absorbed · I can describe how the kidneys remove waste.

5:00

1.Put the parts of the digestive system in the order food passes through them.

Drag each part onto its place in the order, or tap a part and then a place.

Mouth, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, then large intestine. Whatever is left leaves through the rectum and anus.

2.Match each part of the digestive system to its job.

Drag each job onto a part, or tap a job and then a part.

Breaking down starts in the mouth and carries on in the stomach. Nutrients are absorbed in the small intestine and water in the large intestine.

3.Why does food have to be broken down in the digestive system?

Big lumps of food can't get into the blood. Digestion breaks food into small soluble pieces that can pass through the wall of the small intestine into the blood.

4.Which habit is the most common preventable cause of liver disease?

The liver breaks down alcohol and drugs. Alcohol misuse over many years damages the liver, so it is a preventable cause of liver disease.

5.What does excretion mean?

Excretion is removing waste made by the body's cells, such as urea (removed by the kidneys) and carbon dioxide (removed by the lungs).

6.Match each organ to its job in getting rid of waste.

Drag each job onto an organ, or tap a job and then an organ.

The kidneys filter the blood and make urine. It flows down the ureters to the bladder, which stores it. The lungs excrete carbon dioxide.

7.Sort each waste by the organ that gets rid of it.

Drag each waste into its group, or tap it and then the group.

Hint

Ask of each waste: is it a gas, something dissolved in the blood, or food that never got into the blood at all?

Dissolved wastes such as urea and extra salt leave in urine from the kidneys. Carbon dioxide is a gas, breathed out from the lungs. Fibre was never digested, so it leaves through the large intestine as faeces.

Challenge8.A person's kidneys stop working. Why is this dangerous?

Hint

What do the kidneys normally take out of the blood? What would happen to it if they stopped?

The kidneys filter urea, extra salt and extra water out of the blood. If they stop, these build up and poison the body. People with kidney failure need a dialysis machine to clean their blood, or a kidney transplant.
