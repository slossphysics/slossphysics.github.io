BGE · Fourth level · Biological systems · SCN 4-12a

Homeostasis

LI: I am learning how the body keeps conditions inside it steady.

SC: I can describe how the body responds when it is too hot or too cold · I can explain how the kidneys control water · I can explain how insulin controls blood glucose.

5:00

1.What does homeostasis mean?

Homeostasis keeps things such as body temperature, water and blood glucose at steady levels, so the body can work properly.

2.What is the normal temperature inside a healthy human body?

Normal body temperature is about 37 °C. Your skin can be cooler, but the inside of your body stays close to 37 °C.

3.The body responds to changes outside it (external) and inside it (internal). Sort these changes.

Drag each change into its group, or tap it and then the group.

External changes happen in your surroundings, such as the air temperature. Internal changes happen inside the body, such as the amount of water or glucose in the blood.

4.Sort these responses: does the body do each one when it is too hot or when it is too cold?

Drag each response into its group, or tap it and then the group.

Hint

Blood carries heat. If more blood flows near the skin, does the body lose more heat or less?

When too hot, the body sweats and sends more blood near the skin to lose heat. When too cold, it shivers to release heat, keeps blood away from the skin, and raises its hairs to trap a layer of air.

5.How does sweating cool you down?

Hint

How do you feel when you step out of a swimming pool on a windy day, while the water dries off you?

Liquid sweat needs heat energy to evaporate. It takes that energy from your skin, so you cool down. This is why sweating works badly on a humid day, when sweat can't evaporate easily.

6.On a hot day you sweat a lot and drink very little. What do your kidneys do?

When the body is short of water, the kidneys keep back as much water as they can. They still remove urea, so the urine is small in amount and dark.

7.After a meal, the level of glucose in the blood rises. Which hormone does the pancreas release to bring it back down?

Insulin from the pancreas helps glucose leave the blood, mostly to be stored in the liver and muscles. This brings blood glucose back to normal.

8.The graph shows blood glucose after a meal for two people. One of them has type 1 diabetes, so their body can't make insulin. Which line is theirs?

Hint

Insulin brings blood glucose down. What would happen to the glucose if there were no insulin?

Without insulin, glucose can't leave the blood easily, so line B stays high. People with type 1 diabetes inject insulin or use an insulin pump to bring it down.

Challenge9.The graph shows a person's body temperature. They walk into a cold room at the time marked. Which statement best explains the shape of the graph?

Hint

The room stays cold the whole time. So what could make the temperature go back up?

A small drop is detected, and the body responds by shivering, narrowing blood vessels near the skin and raising hairs. This brings the temperature back to normal. That is how homeostasis works.
