BGE · Second and Third level · Biological systems · SCN 2-12a, SCN 3-12a

Organ systems

LI: I am learning how organs work together in organ systems to keep me alive.

SC: I can name the main organs in each system · I can say what each system does · I can explain how two systems work together.

5:00

1.Sort these organs into their organ systems.

Drag each organ into its system, or tap it and then the system.

Respiratory: windpipe and lungs. Circulatory: heart and blood vessels. Digestive: stomach and intestines. Skeletal: bones such as the skull and ribcage.

2.Match each organ system to its main job.

Drag each job onto its system, or tap a job and then a system.

Each system has its own job, but none of them could keep you alive on its own.

3.Which list goes from the smallest to the largest?

Cells group into tissues, tissues make up an organ, and organs work together in an organ system.

4.Which organs does the ribcage protect?

The ribcage wraps round the chest and protects the heart and lungs. The skull protects the brain.

5.Tap all the jobs of the skeleton. There are 3.

The skeleton supports the body, protects organs such as the brain and heart, and works with muscles to let us move.

6.Tap all the things that help to keep bones strong. There are 2.

Calcium in the diet and weight-bearing exercise both help to build strong bones, which lowers the risk of breaks and osteoporosis later in life.

7.Oxygen gets into the body and then travels to the cells. Which two systems does it use, in order?

The respiratory system takes oxygen into the blood in the lungs. The circulatory system then carries it in the blood to every cell.

8.Match each health problem to the system it mainly affects.

Drag each problem onto its system, or tap a problem and then a system.

Hint

Think about which organ each problem damages: the airways, the bones, the heart or the liver. Then think about which system that organ belongs to.

Asthma narrows the airways (respiratory). Osteoporosis weakens bones (skeletal). Heart disease affects the heart (circulatory). The liver is part of the digestive system.

Challenge9.A muscle cell in your leg needs glucose (from food) and oxygen (from the air). Which system do both of them travel through to reach the cell?

Hint

Glucose and oxygen enter the body in different places. What do both of them have to get into before they can reach your leg?

Glucose enters the blood from the small intestine and oxygen enters it in the lungs. The circulatory system then carries both to every cell. This is why the systems depend on each other.
