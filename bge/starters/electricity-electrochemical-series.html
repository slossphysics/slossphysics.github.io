Electrochemical series

LI: I am learning to use the electrochemical series to predict the voltage of a cell.

SC: I can put metals in order from voltage results · I can say which pair of metals gives the biggest voltage · I can place a new metal in the series.

5:00

1.What is the electrochemical series?

The electrochemical series puts metals in order, using the voltage each one gives when it is paired with another metal in a cell.

2.Which of these pairs would make a working cell in salt water? Tap all 3.

A cell needs two different metals in an electrolyte. Any two different metals give some voltage.

Magnesium (1.75 V), zinc (0.95 V), iron (0.70 V), lead (0.35 V). The metal with the biggest voltage is furthest from copper in the series.
