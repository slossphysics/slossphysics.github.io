Resistance and current

B, E, A, D, C. Look at the resistances in that order: 6, 8, 15, 22, 40 Ω. The smallest resistance gave the biggest current.

2.Why did resistor C give the smallest current?

Resistance opposes the current. C has the biggest resistance, so the least current gets through.

Challenge4.A new resistor, F, has a resistance of 30 Ω. In the same circuit, where would its current fit?

30 Ω is between D (22 Ω) and C (40 Ω), so F's current is between theirs: smaller than D's, bigger than C's.
