BGE · Fourth level · Forces, electricity and waves · SCN 4-07b

Balanced and unbalanced forces

LI: I am learning how balanced and unbalanced forces affect the way an object moves.

SC: I can say what balanced and unbalanced forces do · I can work out the unbalanced force from an arrow diagram · I can explain why a moving object does not need a force to keep moving.

5:00

1.A box is sitting still on the floor. Two pupils pull it in opposite directions, with the forces shown. What does the box do?

The forces are equal and opposite, so they are balanced. A still object with balanced forces stays still.

2.Sort each card: does it happen when the forces on an object are balanced, or unbalanced?

Drag each card into its group, or tap a card and then the group.

Hint

Ask: is the speed or the direction changing? If nothing about the motion changes, the forces are balanced.

Balanced forces: the object stays still, or keeps moving at a steady speed in a straight line (the motion does not change). Unbalanced forces: the object speeds up, slows down or changes direction.

3.Two forces act on a box, as shown. Calculate the size of the unbalanced force on the box.

N

You've added the forces. They act in opposite directions, so one cancels out part of the other.

Hint

The forces point in opposite directions. How much bigger is one than the other?

The forces act in opposite directions, so take the smaller from the bigger: 50 − 30 = 20 N to the right.

4.Two forces act on a trolley, as shown. What is the unbalanced force on the trolley?

Opposite directions, so subtract: 40 − 12 = 28 N. The bigger force points right, so the unbalanced force is 28 N to the right.

5.A car moves along a flat, straight road at a steady 20 m/s. The engine gives a forward force of 600 N. What is the total backwards force from friction and air resistance?

Hint

The speed is not changing. What does that tell you about the forces?

A steady speed in a straight line means the forces are balanced, so friction and air resistance add up to 600 N. A moving object does not need an extra forward force to keep moving.

6.A space probe is in deep space, far from any planet or star. It is moving at 15 000 m/s when its engines switch off. There is no air and no friction out there. What happens next?

Hint

Are there any forces on the probe at all? What does an object do when there is no unbalanced force on it?

With no unbalanced force, the motion does not change: the probe keeps moving at the same speed in a straight line. You do not need a force to keep something moving, only to speed it up, slow it down or change its direction.

7.Which of these have an unbalanced force acting on them? Tap all that apply.

Hint

Look for anything that is speeding up, slowing down or changing direction.

Unbalanced forces change the motion. The car is speeding up, the bus is slowing down and the cyclist is changing direction. The book, the parachutist and the train have balanced forces.

8.Two pupils push a heavy crate to the right. Friction acts to the left. Calculate the size of the unbalanced force on the crate.

N

You've added all three forces. Friction acts the opposite way, so it should be taken away.

That's the total push to the right. Friction acts the other way, so take it away.

You've subtracted the two pushes. They both act to the right, so add them together first.

Hint

First add up the forces that act in the same direction. Then compare with the force acting the other way.

Forces to the right: 30 + 25 = 55 N. Force to the left: 40 N. Unbalanced force = 55 − 40 = 15 N to the right.

Challenge9.A skydiver with a weight of 700 N jumps from a plane. At one moment the air resistance on her is 250 N. As she falls faster, the air resistance increases, until she falls at a steady speed. What is the air resistance on her when she is falling at a steady speed?

N

That's the air resistance earlier, while she is still speeding up. What must it be once her speed stops changing?

That's the unbalanced force at the moment the air resistance is 250 N. At a steady speed there is no unbalanced force.

At a steady speed the unbalanced force is zero, but the air resistance itself isn't. It has to match something.

You've added the forces. At a steady speed the forces are balanced.

Hint

Falling at a steady speed means her motion is not changing. What does that tell you about the upwards and downwards forces?

A steady speed means the forces are balanced, so the air resistance upwards equals her weight downwards: 700 N. She does not stop: she keeps falling, but at a constant speed.
