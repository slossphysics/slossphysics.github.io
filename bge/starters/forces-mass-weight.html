BGE · Third level · Forces, electricity and waves · SCN 3-08a

Mass and weight

LI: I am learning the difference between mass and weight.

SC: I can explain that weight is a force caused by gravity, measured in newtons · I can use W = mg · I can set out my working: relationship, substitution, answer with unit.

5:00

1.The arrow shows the weight of a ball held above the Earth. What is weight?

Weight is a force: the pull of a planet's gravity on an object. Like all forces, it is measured in newtons (N).

2.In W = mg, match each symbol to its quantity and unit.

Drag each symbol onto its quantity, or tap a symbol and then its quantity.

W is weight (N), m is mass (kg) and g is gravitational field strength (N/kg).

3.Does each statement describe mass or weight? Sort them.

Drag each card into its group, or tap a card and then the group.

Hint

Mass is how much stuff is in an object. Weight is a pull from gravity.

Mass is the amount of matter, in kilograms, and stays the same everywhere. Weight is the force of gravity, in newtons, measured with a newton meter. It changes when gravity changes, for example on the Moon.

4.A bag of potatoes has a mass of 5 kg. Calculate its weight on Earth, where g = 9.8 N/kg.

N

You've divided the mass by g. Weight is mass multiplied by g.

You've divided g by the mass. Weight is mass multiplied by g.

You've added. Weight is mass multiplied by g.

  • W = mg
  • W = 5 × 9.8
  • W = 49 N

5.Spot the mistake. A pupil works out the weight of a 60 kg person on Earth, where g = 9.8 N/kg:

Weight is a force, so its unit is the newton: W = 588 N, not 588 kg.

6.True or false? "Mass and weight are the same thing. They are just two words for how heavy something is." Choose the answer with the right reason.

False. Mass is the amount of matter in an object, in kilograms. Weight is the force of gravity on it, in newtons. On Earth a 1 kg bag has a weight of 9.8 N.

7.An apple has a mass of 0.1 kg. Calculate its weight on Earth, where g = 9.8 N/kg.

N

You've divided the mass by g. Weight is mass multiplied by g.

You've multiplied 9.8 by 10 instead of by 0.1. Multiplying by 0.1 makes a number smaller.

You've added. Weight is mass multiplied by g.

  • W = mg
  • W = 0.1 × 9.8
  • W = 0.98 N

8.Which has the bigger weight on Earth: 2 kg of feathers or 2 kg of iron?

Both have a mass of 2 kg and the same g, so both have the same weight: W = 2 × 9.8 = 19.6 N.

Challenge9.A suitcase hangs from a newton meter on Earth. The reading is 196 N. Calculate the mass of the suitcase. (g = 9.8 N/kg)

kg

You've multiplied. The reading is the weight: rearrange W = mg to get m on its own.

You've divided g by the weight. Rearrange W = mg to get m on its own.

Hint

A newton meter measures weight. In W = mg, m is being multiplied by g. What do you do to both sides to get m on its own?

  • m = W / g
  • m = 196 / 9.8
  • m = 20 kg
