Measuring speed

LI: I am learning to measure and calculate the speed of an object.

SC: I can use v = d / t and set out my working · I can choose the right unit for a speed · I can compare ways of measuring speed and explain why light gates can be more accurate than a stopwatch.

5:00

1.In v = d / t, match each symbol to its quantity and unit.

Drag each symbol onto its quantity, or tap a symbol and then its quantity.

Hint

Two of the symbols are the first letter of their quantity.

v is speed (m/s), d is distance (m) and t is time (s).

2.Which relationship is used to calculate speed?

Speed = distance ÷ time: v = d / t. It tells you how many metres are covered each second.

3.A pupil runs 100 m in 20 s. Calculate their average speed.

m/s

You've divided the time by the distance. It's distance divided by time.

You've multiplied. Speed is distance divided by time.

  • v = d / t
  • v = 100 / 20
  • v = 5 m/s

4.A trolley with a card on top rolls through a light gate. The light gate is connected to a timer. Which two measurements are needed to work out the trolley's speed?

Hint

Speed needs a distance and a time. While the card passes through, it blocks the light beam. What distance does the trolley move in that time?

The timer measures how long the card blocks the beam. In that time the trolley moves forward one card length. Then v = card length / time.

5.Pupils time a toy car between two marks 1 m apart using a stopwatch. Why can light gates give a more accurate time?

With a stopwatch, the person's reaction time adds an error at the start and the stop. A light gate starts and stops the timer automatically as the beam is blocked and unblocked, so there is no reaction time.

6.To measure speed you need a distance and a time. Sort the equipment by what it measures.

Drag each card into its group, or tap a card and then the group.

Distance: metre stick, trundle wheel, measuring tape. Time: stopwatch, phone timer, and a light gate connected to a timer.

7.A car travels 90 km in 2 hours. Using v = d / t, a pupil gets the answer 45. What is the unit?

Hint

The unit of speed is the distance unit "per" the time unit. What units were used here?

Kilometres divided by hours gives kilometres per hour (km/h). Use metres and seconds to get m/s.

8.Spot the mistake. A cyclist travels 600 m in 2 minutes. A pupil writes:

Hint

For an answer in m/s, the distance must be in metres and the time in seconds.

The time must be in seconds: 2 minutes = 120 s, so v = 600 / 120 = 5 m/s.

Challenge9.A card 5 cm long on a trolley blocks a light gate's beam for 0.25 s. Calculate the speed of the trolley in m/s.

m/s

That's the speed in cm/s. Change 5 cm into metres first.

You've multiplied, and the length is still in cm. Change 5 cm into metres, then divide by the time.

You've multiplied. Speed is distance divided by time.

Hint

There are 100 cm in 1 m. Change the card length into metres before you substitute.

  • d = 5 cm = 0.05 m
  • v = d / t
  • v = 0.05 / 0.25
  • v = 0.2 m/s
