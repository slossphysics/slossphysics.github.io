BGE · Fourth level · Forces, electricity and waves · SCN 4-07b

Force, mass and acceleration

LI: I am learning how the unbalanced force on an object, its mass and its acceleration are linked.

SC: I can use F = ma · I can set out my working: relationship, substitution, answer with unit · I can explain why a bigger mass gives a smaller acceleration for the same force.

5:00

1.Which relationship links unbalanced force, mass and acceleration?

Unbalanced force = mass × acceleration: F = ma. This is Newton's second law.

2.Match each symbol in F = ma to its quantity and unit.

Drag each symbol onto its quantity, or tap a symbol and then its quantity.

F is the unbalanced force (N), m is the mass (kg) and a is the acceleration (m/s²).

3.A 5 kg trolley accelerates at 3 m/s². Calculate the unbalanced force on the trolley.

N

You've divided the mass by the acceleration. F = ma means mass times acceleration.

You've divided the acceleration by the mass. F = ma means mass times acceleration.

You've added. F = ma means mass times acceleration.

  • F = ma
  • F = 5 × 3
  • F = 15 N

4.The same unbalanced force of 10 N pulls two trolleys along a smooth bench. Which trolley has the bigger acceleration?

Hint

Think about pushing an empty shopping trolley and a full one with the same force.

For the same force, a bigger mass gives a smaller acceleration. Using a = F / m: trolley A has 10 / 2 = 5 m/s² and trolley B has 10 / 4 = 2.5 m/s². Trolley A accelerates more.

5.Spot the mistake. A 4 kg box accelerates at 6 m/s². A pupil calculates the unbalanced force:

The working is right up to the unit. F is a force, so the answer is F = 24 N, not 24 kg. Kilograms are the unit of mass.

6.A go-kart and its driver have a total mass of 50 kg. The unbalanced force on the go-kart is 200 N. Calculate its acceleration.

m/s²

You've divided the mass by the force. Rearrange F = ma to get a on its own: a = F / m.

You've multiplied. That would be the force for an acceleration of 200 m/s². Rearrange F = ma to get a on its own.

Hint

You know F and m. In F = ma, a is being multiplied by m. What do you do to both sides to get a on its own?

  • F = ma
  • 200 = 50 × a
  • a = 4 m/s²

7.A 2 kg trolley pulled by an unbalanced force of 10 N has an acceleration of 5 m/s². Sort each trolley below: is its acceleration more or less than 5 m/s²?

Drag each card into its group, or tap a card and then the group.

Hint

For each card, work out a = F / m. Or think: more force speeds it up more, more mass speeds it up less.

Using a = F / m: 1 kg and 10 N is 10 m/s², 2 kg and 20 N is 10 m/s², 1 kg and 8 N is 8 m/s² (all more than 5 m/s²). 4 kg and 10 N is 2.5 m/s², 5 kg and 20 N is 4 m/s², 2 kg and 6 N is 3 m/s² (all less than 5 m/s²).

8.An unbalanced force of 600 N gives a motorbike and its rider an acceleration of 2 m/s². Calculate the total mass of the motorbike and rider.

kg

You've multiplied. Rearrange F = ma to get m on its own: m = F / a.

You've divided the acceleration by the force. Rearrange F = ma to get m on its own: m = F / a.

Hint

You know F and a. In F = ma, m is being multiplied by a. What do you do to both sides to get m on its own?

  • F = ma
  • 600 = m × 2
  • m = 300 kg

Challenge9.An 800 kg car has a forward engine force of 3000 N. Friction and air resistance together are 600 N. Calculate the car's acceleration.

m/s²

You've used the engine force only. F in F = ma is the unbalanced force: take away the backwards force first.

You've added the two forces. They act in opposite directions, so the unbalanced force is the difference.

You've used only the backwards force. Find the unbalanced force first: forward force minus backwards force.

Hint

F in F = ma is the unbalanced force. Work that out from the two arrows first.

Unbalanced force = 3000 − 600 = 2400 N.

  • F = ma
  • 2400 = 800 × a
  • a = 3 m/s²
