BGE · Second and Fourth level · Biological systems · SCN 2-14a, SCN 4-14a

Life cycles

LI: I am learning how plants and animals grow, develop and reproduce.

SC: I can describe pollination, fertilisation and germination · I can describe how the five vertebrate groups reproduce · I can compare two life cycles.

5:00

1.Match each event in a flowering plant's life cycle to its name. The names are in the order they happen.

Drag each event onto its name, or tap an event and then its name.

Hint

Pollination and fertilisation are not the same thing. One is the pollen arriving; the other is what happens afterwards, when the cells join.

Pollination moves pollen (the male cell) to the stigma. Fertilisation happens when the male and female cells' genetic information fuses. The fertilised ovule becomes a seed, which later germinates.

2.After fertilisation, the ovule becomes a seed. What does the ovary around it become?

The ovary ripens into the fruit, which holds the seeds. An apple is a ripened ovary, and its pips are the seeds.

3.A seed is planted in soil. Tap all the things it needs from its surroundings to germinate. There are 3.

A seed needs water, oxygen and warmth from its surroundings. It also uses the food store inside the seed until it can make its own food.

4.Sort these animals into vertebrates and invertebrates.

Drag each one into its group, or tap it and then the group.

Vertebrates have a backbone: fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals. Invertebrates have no backbone, for example insects, spiders and snails.

5.Match each vertebrate group to how its young start life.

Drag each description onto its group, or tap a description and then its group.

Hint

Two groups lay spawn in water. Which one has young that change shape as they grow, like a tadpole turning into a frog?

Fish and amphibians lay spawn in water. Reptiles lay leathery eggs and birds lay hard-shelled eggs. Most mammals give birth to live young.

6.A frog's life cycle goes spawn, tadpole, froglet, adult frog. What is the name for this big change in body shape?

Metamorphosis is a complete change in body shape. A tadpole lives in water and has a tail and gills; the adult frog has legs and lungs and can live on land.

7.A ladybird's life cycle goes egg, larva, pupa, adult. A human's goes baby, child, teenager, adult. Which statement compares them correctly?

A ladybird goes through metamorphosis: the larva looks nothing like the adult. A human is born looking like a small version of an adult and grows without changing shape completely.

8.The table shows roughly how many eggs or young each animal has at one time. Why does a cod produce so many more than a blackbird?

AnimalEggs or young at one time
Cod (fish)4 000 000 eggs
Frog (amphibian)2000 eggs
Blackbird (bird)4 eggs
Human (mammal)1 baby
Hint

Think about what happens to fish spawn floating in the sea, and what a blackbird does with its eggs and chicks.

Animals that give little or no care produce huge numbers of eggs, because most are eaten or die. Animals that care for their young have far fewer, but each one has a much better chance of surviving.

Challenge9.A sunflower and a frog both reproduce sexually. Which statement about both of their life cycles is true?

Hint

Sexual reproduction always involves the same key step, whether it happens in a flower or in a pond.

In all sexual reproduction, a male and a female sex cell join at fertilisation. In the sunflower this makes a seed; in the frog it makes an egg that develops in the spawn.
