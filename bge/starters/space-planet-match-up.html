Sun

Drag the clue onto the right planet, or tap the planet.

Who am I?I am the closest planet to the Sun.

Venus is very close, but one planet is even closer.

Mercury is closest to the Sun. It is also the smallest planet.

Who am I?I am the hottest planet. My thick clouds trap the heat.

Mercury is closest to the Sun, but it has no thick clouds to trap the heat. Which planet is wrapped in cloud?

Venus is about 465 °C: hot enough to melt lead.

Who am I?I am the only planet where we know there is life.

Scientists are searching Mars for signs of life, but none has been found yet.

Earth is the only planet with liquid water on its surface.

Who am I?I am the Red Planet. My dust has rusty iron in it.

Jupiter has a Great Red Spot, but it is a gas giant. The Red Planet is small and rocky.

Mars also has the tallest volcano in the Solar System.

Who am I?I am the biggest planet. More than 1000 Earths would fit inside me.

Saturn's rings make it look wide, but the planet itself is smaller than one other.

Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than the Earth.

Who am I?My huge, bright rings are made of chunks of ice and rock.

Saturn is less dense than water. It would float in a giant bath!

Who am I?I spin on my side, like a ball rolling along.

Neptune is the other blue giant, but it spins upright. Try its neighbour.

Uranus was probably knocked onto its side by a huge crash long ago.

Who am I?I am the windiest planet, and the furthest from the Sun.

Uranus is a long way out, but one planet is even further.

Neptune's winds reach 2000 km/h. One orbit of the Sun takes 165 years.
