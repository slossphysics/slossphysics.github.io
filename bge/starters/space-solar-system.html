BGE · Second level · Planet Earth · SCN 2-06a

The Solar System

LI: I am learning about the planets in our Solar System and how they compare.

SC: I can put the eight planets in order from the Sun · I can sort them into rocky and giant planets · I can compare their size, year, day, temperature and moons.

5:00

1.Which planet is closest to the Sun?

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. It is also the smallest planet.

2.Which planet is the largest?

Jupiter is the largest planet. More than 1000 Earths could fit inside it.

3.Put the planets in order from the Sun. One of these is not a planet.

Drag each name to its place, or tap a name and then its place.

Hint

The four small, rocky planets are closest to the Sun. The four giant planets come after them.

Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune. Pluto was called a planet until 2006. It is now called a dwarf planet because it is so small.

4.Sort the planets into rocky planets and giant planets.

Drag each planet into its group, or tap a planet and then the group.

The four inner planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) are small and rocky. The four outer planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune) are giants made mostly of gas, with no solid surface to stand on. Uranus and Neptune are sometimes called ice giants.

5.Planets P and Q both orbit the Sun. Which planet has the longer year?

Hint

A planet's year is the time it takes to go once around the Sun. Compare the two orbits.

A year is one orbit of the Sun. Q is further out, so it has further to travel and it moves more slowly: its year is longer. Earth's year is about 365 days; Neptune's is about 165 Earth years.

6.True or false? Venus is the hottest planet, even though Mercury is closer to the Sun.

True. Venus has a very thick atmosphere that traps heat, so its surface is about 460 °C, hotter than Mercury. Mercury has almost no atmosphere, so its heat escapes and its nights are freezing.

7.A day is the time a planet takes to spin once. Earth's day is 24 hours. Which of these is true?

Each planet spins at its own speed. Jupiter spins fastest (a day of about 10 hours), while Venus spins so slowly that its day is longer than its year. Day length does not follow the order from the Sun, but year length does.

8.Match each planet to its description.

Drag each planet onto its description, or tap a planet and then its description.

Earth has one Moon and life. Saturn has bright rings and many moons (Jupiter has many moons too). Mercury is the smallest planet; it and Venus have no moons. Mars is red because of rusty (iron oxide) dust.

Challenge9.A space probe visits the planet that is further from the Sun than Mars, but closer than Saturn. Which of these are true about that planet? Tap all that apply.

Hint

First work out which planet it is: list the planets in order and find the one between Mars and Saturn.

The planet is Jupiter, the fifth planet. It is the largest planet, a giant made mostly of gas, and it has many moons. It is further from the Sun than Earth, so its year is longer (about 12 Earth years). It is closer to the Sun than Neptune, so it is warmer.
