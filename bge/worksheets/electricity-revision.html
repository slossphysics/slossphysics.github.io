BGE · Third level · Forces, electricity and waves · SCN 3-09a

Electricity revision

Write your answers in your jotter. Give the unit with every number, and use full sentences when a question says explain. Stuck? Look back at the rules table in your jotter.

AQuick recall Everyone. Short answers are fine.

  1. 1What is the unit of current? What is the unit of voltage?
  2. 2Which meter measures voltage? Is it connected in series or in parallel?
  3. 3Draw the circuit symbols for a cell, a lamp, a switch and an ammeter.
  4. 4What is the difference between a series circuit and a parallel circuit?
  5. 5What is the unit of resistance?

BRead the meters Write the missing meter reading, with its unit.

1Two lamps in series. Ammeter A₁ reads 0.20 A. What does ammeter A₂ read?

2Two different lamps in series on a 6.0 V battery. Voltmeter P reads 2.5 V. What does voltmeter Q read?

3Two lamps in parallel. Ammeter A₁ reads 0.90 A and ammeter A₂ reads 0.40 A. What does ammeter A₃ read?

4Two lamps in parallel on a 4.5 V battery. What does the voltmeter read?

CResistance Give a reason for each answer.

  1. 1A lamp is in series with a 5 Ω resistor. The resistor is swapped for a 20 Ω resistor. What happens to the current? What happens to the brightness of the lamp?
  2. 2Which has more resistance: a long wire or a short wire? A thin wire or a thick wire?
  3. 3An ammeter in a circuit reads 0.30 A. Then a resistor is added in series. Which of these could the new reading be: 0.45 A, 0.30 A or 0.12 A? Explain your choice.

DExplain it Everyone. Full sentences.

  1. 1Ellie says: “The current is bigger before a lamp than after it, because the lamp uses some of the current up.” Explain what is wrong with what Ellie says.
  2. 2When one headlight on a car blows, the other headlight stays on. Are the headlights wired in series or in parallel? Explain how you know.
  3. 3A torch has three 1.5 V cells joined in series. What is the total voltage?

EFinished? Keep going Harder questions for when you have finished A to D.

  1. 1Three identical lamps are in series on a 6.0 V battery. What is the voltage across each lamp?
  2. 2Draw a circuit with a cell, two lamps in parallel and one switch. The switch must turn off only one of the lamps.
  3. 3In a parallel circuit, the battery current is 1.5 A. There are three identical lamps, one in each branch. What is the current through each lamp?
  4. 4Sam’s lamp is too bright. Give two different ways Sam could change the circuit to make the lamp dimmer.
