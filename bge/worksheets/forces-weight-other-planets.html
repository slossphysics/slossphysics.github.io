Weight on other planets

Write your answers in your jotter. For every calculation, write W = m × g, then put in the numbers, then give the answer with its unit (N). Use the table for g.

gGravity on other planets Gravitational field strength, g, in newtons per kilogram (N/kg)

MercuryVenusEarthMarsJupiterSaturnUranusNeptuneThe Moon
491042399112

W = m × g   W = weight (N)  ·  m = mass (kg)  ·  g = gravitational field strength (N/kg)

AUse the table Everyone. Short answers are fine.

  1. 1What is g on Mars?
  2. 2Which planet has the strongest gravity?
  3. 3Which planet has the same g as Mercury?
  4. 4Is gravity on the Moon stronger or weaker than on Earth?

BWork out the weight Everyone. Show your working: formula, numbers, answer with unit.

  1. 1A bag of shopping has a mass of 5 kg. What is its weight on Earth?
  2. 2A girl has a mass of 30 kg. What would her weight be on the Moon?
  3. 3An astronaut has a mass of 80 kg. What would his weight be on Mars?
  4. 4A dog has a mass of 12 kg. What would its weight be on Neptune?
  5. 5A football has a mass of 0.5 kg. What would its weight be on Jupiter?
  6. 6A car has a mass of 1500 kg. What would its weight be on Venus?

CMass or weight? Everyone. Full sentences.

  1. 1An astronaut has a mass of 70 kg on Earth. What is her mass on the Moon? Explain your answer.
  2. 2Ruby says: “You weigh less on the Moon because there is less of you there.” Explain what is wrong with what Ruby says.
  3. 3A 10 kg rock is taken to Earth, Mars and Neptune. Where would it weigh the most? Use the table to explain.
  4. 4Astronauts on the Moon can jump much higher than on Earth. Explain why.

DFinished? Keep going Harder questions for when you have finished A to C.

  1. 1A box has a mass of 20 kg. On one planet it weighs 180 N. Which planet could it be on? (There is more than one answer.)
  2. 2A rover weighs 400 N on Mars. What is its mass?
  3. 3A rucksack weighs 90 N on Earth. What is its mass? What would it weigh on Jupiter?
  4. 4A space probe travels far out into space, a long way from any planet or star. What happens to its mass? What happens to its weight?
