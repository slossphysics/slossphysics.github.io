Nuclear power: for and against

LI: I am learning to weigh up the advantages and disadvantages of generating electricity using nuclear fuel.

SC: I can sort statements into for and against · I can describe how a nuclear power station generates electricity · I can compare nuclear power with other ways of generating electricity.

5:00

1.Sort each statement about nuclear power stations: is it for (an advantage) or against (a disadvantage)?

Drag each statement into its group, or tap it and then the group.

For: no carbon dioxide while running, lots of energy from a little fuel, and reliable in any weather. Against: long-lasting radioactive waste, high cost, the risk of an accident, and fuel that will run out.

2.Nuclear power stations use uranium as fuel. Is uranium a renewable or a non-renewable energy source?

Uranium is mined from rocks and can't be replaced, so it is non-renewable. It will run out one day, like coal, oil and gas.

3.The nuclear fuel in a power station gets very hot. What is this heat used for?

Hint

A nuclear power station works like a coal or gas one. Only the way it makes heat is different.

The heat boils water into steam. The steam spins a turbine, which turns a generator to make electricity, just like in a coal or gas power station.

4.Scotland's nuclear power stations, such as Torness in East Lothian and Hunterston in Ayrshire, were built on the coast. Why?

After the steam has turned the turbines, it has to be cooled back into water. Power stations use huge amounts of seawater for cooling.

5.True or false? A nuclear power station gives out carbon dioxide while it is generating electricity.

Nothing is burned, so no carbon dioxide is given out while it runs. Some carbon dioxide is made when the station is built and when uranium is mined, but far less than from burning coal or gas.

6.What is the biggest problem with the waste from a nuclear power station?

Some nuclear waste stays radioactive for thousands of years. It has to be sealed and stored safely, away from people and water supplies, for a very long time.

7.Which of these is an advantage of a nuclear power station compared with a wind farm?

A wind farm only works when the wind blows. A nuclear power station keeps generating day and night, whatever the weather.

Challenge8.A town must choose between a new gas power station and a new nuclear power station. Which statement is completely true?

Hint

Check both halves of each statement. One wrong half makes the whole statement wrong.

Burning gas gives out carbon dioxide, which adds to global warming. Nuclear power gives out almost none, but makes radioactive waste that must be stored for thousands of years. Each choice has a different risk.
