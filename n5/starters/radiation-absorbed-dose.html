Absorbed dose

LI: I am learning to calculate absorbed dose.

1.What does absorbed dose measure?

Absorbed dose is the energy absorbed per kilogram of the tissue (or other material) that absorbs it. One gray is one joule absorbed by each kilogram.

5.Two workers each absorb 0.30 J of energy from radiation. Worker A absorbs it all in one hand, of mass 0.50 kg. Worker B absorbs it spread evenly over their whole body, of mass 75 kg. Which tissue receives the greater absorbed dose?

Absorbed dose is energy per kilogram. The same 0.30 J in a smaller mass gives a bigger dose: 0.60 Gy to the hand, but only 0.0040 Gy to the whole body.
