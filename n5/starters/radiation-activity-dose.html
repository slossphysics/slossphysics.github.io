1.What is the unit of activity?

Activity is measured in becquerels (Bq). An activity of 1 Bq means that one nucleus decays each second.

7.A radioactive tracer is injected into a patient. A detector outside the body follows where it goes. Which type of radiation should the tracer give out, and why?

Hint

The detector is outside the body. Which radiation is penetrating enough to get out through the patient's tissue?

A tracer gives out gamma radiation. It is penetrating enough to pass out of the body to the detector, and it causes the least ionisation on the way, so it does the least harm to the patient.

8.What is meant by the half-life of a radioactive source?

The half-life of a radioactive source is the time taken for its activity to halve.
