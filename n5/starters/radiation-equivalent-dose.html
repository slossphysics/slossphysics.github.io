Equivalent dose

1.Why do we need equivalent dose as well as absorbed dose?

The same absorbed dose does more biological harm if it comes from some types of radiation than from others. Equivalent dose takes this into account by multiplying the absorbed dose by a radiation weighting factor.

5.One sample of tissue receives an absorbed dose of 1 mGy from alpha radiation (weighting factor 20). An identical sample receives 1 mGy from gamma radiation (weighting factor 1). Which statement is correct?

Both samples absorb the same energy per kilogram, but alpha is far more ionising, so it does more biological harm. Its equivalent dose is 1 × 20 = 20 mSv, compared with 1 × 1 = 1 mSv for gamma.
