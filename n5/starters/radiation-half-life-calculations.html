1.Fluorine-18 is used in hospital cancer scans. Its half-life is 110 minutes. A sample has an activity of 960 Bq. Calculate its activity after 330 minutes.

2.Cobalt-60 is used in radiotherapy machines. Its half-life is 5 years. A source has an activity of 1600 Bq. Calculate its activity after 20 years.

Bq

You've divided by 4. The activity halves four times: 1600, then 800, and keep going.

That's the activity after one half-life. How many half-lives are there in 20 years?

That's three halvings. How many lots of 5 years fit into 20 years?

That's five halvings. How many lots of 5 years fit into 20 years?

You've divided the activity by the half-life. Work out the number of half-lives first, then halve that many times.

You've divided the activity by the time. Work out the number of half-lives first, then halve that many times.

Hint

Start with the number of half-lives: how many lots of 5 years fit into 20 years? Then write 1600 Bq and draw that many arrows.

Number of half-lives = 20 ÷ 5 = 4

  • 1600 Bq → 800 Bq → 400 Bq → 200 Bq → 100 Bq
  • Activity after 20 years = 100 Bq

3.Sodium-24 is used to find leaks in underground pipes. The activity of a sample falls from 480 Bq to 60 Bq in 45 hours. Calculate the half-life of sodium-24.

hours

That's the number of half-lives. Now share the 45 hours between them.

480 ÷ 60 = 8 is not the number of half-lives. Keep halving 480 until you reach 60, and count the arrows.

You've counted two half-lives. Keep halving 480 until you reach 60, and count the arrows again.

You've counted four half-lives. Keep halving 480 until you reach 60, and count the arrows again.

You've multiplied. The 45 hours is shared between the half-lives.

Hint

Write 480 Bq and keep halving until you reach 60 Bq. Count the arrows. That many half-lives took 45 hours.

  • 480 Bq → 240 Bq → 120 Bq → 60 Bq
  • 3 half-lives = 45 hours
  • Half-life = 45 ÷ 3 = 15 hours
