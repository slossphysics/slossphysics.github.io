Splitting the nucleus

LI: I am recalling what happens in fission and fusion, and how a nuclear power station uses fission.

SC: I can label a fission diagram · I can tell fission from fusion · I can explain a chain reaction · I can describe how a power station makes electricity.

8:00

1.Label the fission diagram. Match each label to its letter.

Drag each label onto its letter, or tap a label and then the letter.

A neutron (A) hits a uranium-235 nucleus (B), which splits into two smaller nuclei (C), releasing energy and 2 or 3 neutrons (D).

2.Which diagram shows nuclear fusion?

B. In fusion, two small nuclei join to form a larger nucleus, releasing energy. A is fission (a large nucleus splits); C is alpha decay.

3.Which of these gets its energy from nuclear fusion?

The Sun. Hydrogen nuclei fuse in its core. There are no fusion power stations yet: Torness uses fission.

4.Put the steps of fission in order.

Drag each sentence to its place, or tap a sentence and then its place.

A neutron hits a uranium-235 nucleus, the nucleus splits into two smaller nuclei, energy and 2 or 3 neutrons are released, and those neutrons hit more nuclei.

5.In this chain reaction, every fission releases 3 neutrons, and each neutron splits another nucleus. How many fissions happen in the next step?

You've added 3. Each of the 9 fissions releases 3 neutrons, so multiply.

That would be 2 neutrons from each fission. In this diagram each fission releases 3.

That's all the fissions so far added together (1 + 3 + 9). How many happen in the next step on its own?

That's one step too far. Look at the 9 fissions: how many neutrons do they release?

Each of the 9 fissions releases 3 neutrons, not 1.

Hint

Look at how the numbers grow: 1, then 3, then 9. What do you multiply by each time?

Another hint

Each of the 9 fissions releases 3 neutrons, and every neutron splits a nucleus. So multiply 9 by 3.

  • 9 fissions × 3 neutrons each = 27 neutrons
  • Each neutron splits a nucleus: 27 fissions

That is a chain reaction: the number of fissions keeps growing.
