Speed, distance and time

In your jotter, three lines every time: the equation, the numbers in, the answer with a unit.

d = v̄t

  1. d is distance, in metres (m)
  2. v̄ is average speed, in m/s
  3. t is time, in seconds (s)

Stuck? Like Usain Bolt

d = v̄t
100 = v̄ × 9.58
v̄ = 10.4 m/s

CReal exam questions From SQA National 5 papers. Each one is worth 3 marks. Carry on here when you have finished A and B.

  1. 1SQA 2025 · wave poolA wave pool is 160 m long. Each wave takes 32 s to travel the length of the pool. Calculate the average speed of the waves. (3)
  2. 2SQA 2017 · speedwayA speedway race is four laps of a 380 m track. The winner completes all four laps in a time of 79 s. Calculate the average speed of the winner. (3)
  3. 3SQA 2024 · triathlonThe last stage of a triathlon is four laps of a 2.5 km course. A triathlete takes 38 minutes to complete this stage. Determine the average speed of the triathlete for this stage. (3)
  4. 4SQA 2019 · quadcopter raceA quadcopter takes 32.5 s to complete a race. A second quadcopter completes the race at an average speed of 1.25 m/s. The distance it travels during the race is 37.0 m. Determine the difference in the times taken by the two quadcopters. (3)
