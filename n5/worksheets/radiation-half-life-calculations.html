CGoing further The same arrows, with a bit more thinking. Carry on here when you have finished A and B.

  1. 1Technetium-99m has a half-life of 6 hours. A hospital's sample has an activity of 1600 Bq at 9 am on Monday. Calculate its activity at 9 am on Tuesday.
  2. 2Iodine-131 has a half-life of 8 days. A sample has an activity of 800 Bq. Calculate how long it takes for the activity to fall to 50 Bq.
  3. 3Caesium-137 has a half-life of 30 years. A soil sample has an activity of 120 Bq today. Calculate the activity it had 60 years ago.
  4. 4Carbon-14 has a half-life of 5730 years. The wood from an ancient boat has 1/4 of the activity of living wood. Calculate the age of the boat.
  5. 5The americium-241 in a smoke detector has a half-life of 432 years. What fraction of its activity is left after 1296 years?
  6. 6Strontium-90 has a half-life of 29 years. Calculate how many years it takes for its activity to fall to 1/16 of its starting value.
