Finding a half-life from a graph

  1. Read the activity at the start.
  2. Halve it.
  3. Go across from that activity to the curve, then down to the time axis.

DHalf-life calculations Set each one out with arrows.

  1. 1Strontium-90 is found in nuclear waste. Its half-life is 29 years. A sample has an activity of 800 Bq. Calculate its activity after 87 years.
  2. 2Gallium-68 is used in cancer scans. Its half-life is 68 minutes. A sample has an activity of 1200 Bq. Calculate its activity after 204 minutes.
  3. 3The activity of a sample of radon-222 falls from 2000 Bq to 250 Bq in 12 days. Calculate the half-life of radon-222.
  4. 4Technetium-99m has a half-life of 6 hours. A sample has an activity of 4000 Bq. Calculate how long it takes for the activity to fall to 250 Bq.
  5. 5Cobalt-60 has a half-life of 5 years. A source has an activity of 100 Bq today. Calculate the activity it had 15 years ago.
  6. 6Polonium-210 has a half-life of 138 days. What fraction of its activity is left after 414 days?
